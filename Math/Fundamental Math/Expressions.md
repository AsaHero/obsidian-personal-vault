In general, expression refers to transforming one's ideas, thoughts, and emotions into words. In terms of mathematics, the expression means transforming words into math language.
So let's start exploring math language. 

The expression are made of **variables, constants, coefficients, and operations**, which are the base of math language, lets explore each.

## Variables
___
Solving a problem involves searching for a solution. In the process, we often encounter unknown factors. Identifying what these factors are can likely lead us to a solution, or at least bring us closer to it.

In mathematics, these unknown factors are called **variables**. Variables are represented by alphabetical symbols, such as $a, b, c, \ldots, x, y, z$. The value of variables are unknown, in fact it can be any number.

## Coefficients
___
Coefficients are the numerical factors that multiply the variables in an expression. For example, in the expression $4x$, 4 is the coefficient, indicating that $x$ is multiplied by 4. Coefficients can be constants, or they can be more complex expressions themselves, but their role is always to scale or modify the variable they are attached to.

## Constant
___
Constants are opposite of variables, the value of those always fixed. So, **constant** - any number or symbol with fixed value. For example: 12, 45, -5, $\pi$ and etc. 

## Operations
___
Operations are the actions that tell us what to do with the variables and constants in an expression. The basic mathematical operations are addition (+), subtraction (-), multiplication (x), and division (/). 


The expressions are very handy tool in mathematics, it allows you describe ton of words just with arithmetic operators, variables and numbers.

Let's see an examples:

1. *Sum of three and seven*: $3+7$

2. Sum of some number to ten: $x + 10$

3. The variable $c$ represents the weight of one circle. To write an expression with $c$ in it that describes 4 circles plus 15 kg: ![[Pasted image 20240222231625.png]]

$$4c + 15$$
