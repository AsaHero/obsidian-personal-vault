##### Problem 1 
Plot a line with slope $\frac{1}{3}$​ that goes through $(−3,−2)$.

**Step 1:** Find y-intercept point of the graph:
General equation of line: $y = mx + c$
$$y = \frac13x + c$$
Let's put values for x, y:
$$\displaylines{-2 = \frac{1}{3}\times-3 + c\\-2=-1+c\\c= -1}$$
y-intercept $(0; -1)$
**Step 2:** Find other point making x = 3:
$$\displaylines{
y=\frac{1}{3}\times3-1\\
y=0
}$$
Second point $(3;0)$
**Step 3:** Draw the graph:
```functionplot
---
title: 
xLabel: x
yLabel: y
bounds: [-5,5,-5,5]
disableZoom: true
grid: true
---
y = 1/3x-1
```
